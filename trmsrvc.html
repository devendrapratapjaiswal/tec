Booking & Payment Terms

Booking Process

  • Booking ke liye advance payment zaroori hai
  • Final menu aur guest count booking ke time confirm hona chahiye
  • Booking confirmation written agreement ke baad hi valid hogi
  • Event se kam se kam 7 din pehle final confirmation zaroori

Payment Structure

  • Advance Payment: Total amount ka 50% booking ke time
  • Balance Payment: Baaki 50% event ke din ya ek din pehle
  • Large events ke liye installment options available hain
  • GST extra hai unless specifically mentioned otherwise

Accepted Payment Methods

  • Cash payment
  • Bank Transfer / NEFT / RTGS
  • UPI Payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Cheque (subject to clearance before event)

Note: Bounced cheque ya failed payments pe additional charges lag sakte hain aur booking cancel ho sakti hai.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Hum samajhte hain ki kabhi kabhi plans change ho jate hain. Isliye humari cancellation policy fair aur transparent hai:

Customer Cancellation

  • 30+ din pehle cancel: Full refund (minus 5% processing fee)
  • 15-30 din pehle cancel: 70% refund
  • 7-14 din pehle cancel: 50% refund
  • 7 din se kam: No refund (advance forfeit hoga)

Date Modification

  • Date change request kam se kam 15 din pehle karni hogi
  • New date availability pe depend karega
  • Ek baar free date change allowed, uske baad nominal charge lagega

Humari Taraf Se Cancellation

Agar kisi unforeseen circumstance (natural disaster, government restrictions, etc.) ki wajah se hum service provide nahi kar paate, toh full refund diya jayega ya alternative date offer ki jayegi.

Food Safety & Hygiene

Food safety humari top priority hai. Hum FSSAI guidelines follow karte hain aur highest hygiene standards maintain karte hain.

Humari Commitment

  • Fresh ingredients ka use - kabhi compromise nahi
  • FSSAI approved kitchen aur processes
  • Trained staff with proper hygiene training
  • Regular quality checks aur temperature monitoring
  • Clean uniforms, gloves, aur hair nets mandatory

Aapki Responsibility

  • Allergies ya dietary restrictions advance mein batayein
  • Leftover food ka proper storage customer ki zimmedari hai
  • Event ke 2-3 ghante baad khana consume karna recommended nahi hai

Allergy Disclaimer: Humara kitchen multiple ingredients handle karta hai. Cross-contamination ka minor risk ho sakta hai. Severe allergies wale guests ke liye please humse pehle baat karein.

Customer Ki Zimmedari

Successful event ke liye customer aur caterer dono ka cooperation zaroori hai. Aapki kuch responsibilities hain:

Event Details

  • Accurate guest count provide karein (±10% variance acceptable)
  • Venue address aur access details clearly batayein
  • Electricity, water, aur basic facilities venue pe available hon
  • Serving area / kitchen space ki arrangement customer karenge

Communication

  • Event coordinator ya contact person designate karein
  • Menu changes kam se kam 5 din pehle communicate karein
  • Any special requirements advance mein discuss karein

Venue Related

  • Venue access event se 3-4 ghante pehle available hona chahiye
  • Safe parking space for our vehicles
  • Venue rules aur restrictions humein batayein

Liability Ki Limits

Hum apni services mein best quality dene ki koshish karte hain, lekin kuch situations mein humari liability limited hoti hai:

Hum Responsible Nahi Hain

  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, riots, government orders)
  • Venue ke electricity ya facility failure se hone wale issues
  • Customer ke guests dwara cause kiya gaya koi damage
  • Leftover food ke improper storage se hone wali problems
  • Undisclosed allergies ya medical conditions

Maximum Liability

Kisi bhi claim mein humari maximum liability paid amount tak limited hogi. Consequential, indirect, ya special damages ke liye hum liable nahi hain.

Insurance

Hum professional liability insurance maintain karte hain. Major events ke liye separate event insurance recommend ki jaati hai.

Home Page Pe Wapas Jayein

Introduction - Shuruwaat

The Entire Caters ("hum", "humara", ya "humari company") mein aapki privacy humari top priority hai. Yeh Privacy Policy batati hai ki jab aap humari website theentirecaters.com ya humari services use karte ho toh hum aapki personal information ko kaise collect, use, disclose aur safeguard karte hain.

Humari services use karke, aap is policy ko accept karte ho. Agar aap is policy se agree nahi karte, toh please humari website use mat karein.

Humara Promise: Aapka data safe hai humare paas! Hum aapki information ko kabhi bhi bina aapki permission ke third parties ko nahi bechte.

Hum Kya Information Collect Karte Hain?

1. Personal Information

Jab aap humse contact karte ho ya booking karte ho, toh hum yeh information le sakte hain:

  • Naam: Aapka full name taaki hum aapko properly address kar sakein
  • Phone Number: Event ki details discuss karne aur updates dene ke liye
  • Email Address: Quotes, confirmations aur important information bhejne ke liye
  • Event Address: Catering service provide karne ke liye venue location
  • Event Details: Date, guest count, menu preferences, special requirements

2. Automatically Collected Information

Jab aap humari website visit karte ho, kuch information automatically collect hoti hai:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns
  • IP Address: Approximate location aur security purposes ke liye
  • Cookies: Website experience better banane ke liye small data files

3. Communication Data

Jab aap humse baat karte ho through phone, email ya WhatsApp:

  • Aapke messages aur inquiries ka record
  • Feedback aur reviews jo aap dete ho
  • Support conversations ka history

Hum Aapki Information Kaise Use Karte Hain?

Aapki information hum sirf legitimate purposes ke liye use karte hain:

  • Service Provide Karna: Aapke event ke liye catering arrange karna
  • Communication: Quotes bhejnaa, bookings confirm karna, updates dena
  • Customization: Aapki preferences ke according menu plan karna
  • Improvement: Humari services aur website ko better banana
  • Marketing: Sirf aapki permission se special offers aur updates bhejnaa
  • Legal Compliance: Laws aur regulations follow karna
  • Security: Fraud prevent karna aur safe transactions ensure karna

Note: Hum aapko kabhi spam nahi karenge! Marketing emails tab hi bhejenge jab aap subscribe karoge, aur unsubscribe ka option hamesha available rahega.

Information Sharing - Kaun Dekh Sakta Hai?

Hum aapki personal information ko third parties ke saath share karne mein bahut careful hain. Yeh sirf specific situations mein hota hai:

  • Service Partners: Jo vendors humari help karte hain event complete karne mein (jaise venue coordinators) - sirf zaroori information
  • Legal Requirements: Agar law require kare ya court order ho
  • Business Transfer: Agar company merge ya acquire ho (bahut rare situation)
  • Your Consent: Jab aap specifically permission do

Hum Kabhi Nahi Karenge: Aapka data advertisers ya data brokers ko sell nahi karenge. Yeh humara pakka promise hai!

Data Security - Aapka Data Safe Hai!

Hum aapki information protect karne ke liye serious measures lete hain:

  • Secure Storage: Aapka data encrypted servers pe store hota hai
  • Access Control: Sirf authorized team members hi data access kar sakte hain
  • Regular Updates: Security measures ko regularly update karte hain
  • Safe Communication: Sensitive information ke liye secure channels use karte hain
  • Staff Training: Humari team ko privacy practices ki training di jaati hai

Lekin yaad rakhein, koi bhi internet transmission 100% secure nahi hai. Hum apna best effort karte hain aapki information protect karne ke liye, but complete security guarantee nahi de sakte.

Cookies - Small Data Files

Humari website cookies use karti hai - yeh chhoti files hain jo aapke browser mein store hoti hain. Yeh humari website ko better work karne mein help karti hain.

Hum Cookies Kyun Use Karte Hain:

  • Essential Cookies: Website properly function karne ke liye zaroori hain
  • Analytics Cookies: Samajhne ke liye ki visitors website kaise use karte hain
  • Preference Cookies: Aapki settings aur preferences yaad rakhne ke liye

Cookies Control Kaise Karein:

Aap apne browser settings mein jaake cookies disable kar sakte ho. Lekin yaad rakhein ki kuch features properly kaam nahi karenge agar cookies off hon.

Aapke Rights - Aap Kya Kar Sakte Ho?

Aapki information pe aapka control hai. Aap yeh kar sakte ho:

  • Access: Jaanein ki humne aapka kya data store kiya hai
  • Correction: Galat information update karwayein
  • Deletion: Apna data delete karwane ki request karein
  • Opt-Out: Marketing communications se unsubscribe karein
  • Portability: Apna data dusre format mein receive karein
  • Objection: Certain processing activities pe objection karein

In rights ko exercise karne ke liye, humse contact karein. Hum 30 days ke andar aapki request pe respond karenge.

Third-Party Links

Humari website pe kuch external links ho sakte hain (jaise social media pages). Yeh websites ki apni alag privacy policies hain aur hum unke liye responsible nahi hain.

Hum recommend karte hain ki aap un websites ki privacy policies bhi padh lein unhe use karne se pehle.

Children's Privacy - Bacchon Ki Privacy

Humari services 18 saal se upar ke logon ke liye designed hain. Hum jaanboojhkar 18 saal se chhote bacchon ki information collect nahi karte.

Agar humein pata chale ki humne accidentally kisi minor ki information collect kar li hai, toh hum use turant delete kar denge. Agar aapko lagta hai ki humne aapke bachche ki information collect ki hai, toh please turant humse contact karein.

Data Retention - Data Kitne Time Rakhte Hain?

Hum aapki information sirf utne time tak rakhte hain jitna zaroori ho:

  • Active Customers: Jab tak aap humari services use karte ho
  • Completed Events: Event ke baad 3 saal tak (legal requirements ke liye)
  • Marketing Data: Jab tak aap subscribed ho ya unsubscribe nahi karte
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated form mein 2 saal tak

Retention period ke baad, hum aapka data securely delete ya anonymize kar dete hain.

Policy Updates - Changes Mein Kaise Jaanenge?

Hum samay-samay pe is Privacy Policy ko update kar sakte hain. Jab bhi koi significant change ho, hum:

  • Website pe updated policy post karenge
  • "Last Updated" date change karenge
  • Major changes ke liye email notification bhej sakte hain

Hum recommend karte hain ki aap regularly is page ko check karein taaki latest information aapko pata rahe.
